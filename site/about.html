About

AgentRoute Arena is an open benchmark for agentic routing — choosing the best agent, tool path, handoff, or clarification strategy across an evolving goal state, across many turns.

— MOTIVATION

Why this benchmark exists

Existing LLM-router benchmarks evaluate single-turn model selection: "which model best answers this prompt?" That framing breaks the moment a task becomes multi-turn. An agentic router must reason over the goal, the state so far, the available capabilities, and the constraints — and decide whether to continue, re-route, split work, escalate, clarify, or stop. There was no open, reproducible benchmark for that decision-making. AgentRoute Arena fills the gap.
