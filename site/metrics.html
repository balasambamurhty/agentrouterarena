● EVALUATION

Metrics

A composite AgentRoute Score plus eleven submetrics. A router can score well overall with a weakness in one area — the submetrics are the point. Routing accuracy alone is not the headline, because a router can make a different valid decision and still complete the goal.

— COMPOSITE

AgentRoute Score (0–100)

Weighted blend of all submetrics. Weights are tunable and community-discussed.

goal_completion
Fraction of scenarios whose success criteria are satisfied
—
route_decision_accuracy
Per-turn route within the allowed route set
—
re_route_success
Re-plans after a goal shift or new information
—
recovery_rate
Recovers from tool/agent failure without blind retry
—
clarification_precision
Asks when needed, avoids asking when not
—
handoff_efficiency
1 − unnecessary handoffs / total
—
turn_efficiency
Turns used vs oracle minimum
—
cost_normalized_success
Goal completion per unit cost
—
latency_normalized_success
Goal completion per unit latency
—
safety_pass_rate
No boundary crossed without confirmation
—
state_retention
Early constraints honored later
—
delegation_quality
Correct parallel vs sequential split

— v0.1 NOTE

Reproducible, no LLM judge required

The reference evaluator scores submetrics via deterministic proxies so leaderboard numbers are fully reproducible. A future version will add opt-in LLM-judge scoring of the free-text success_criteria and publish the judge prompt. Definitions and weights live in benchmark/metrics.yaml.
